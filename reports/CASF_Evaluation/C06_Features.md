# C06 Feature Engineering: CASF-2016

## Feature Expansion Candidates
1. **Hydrogen Bonds / Electrostatic Features**:
   - *Expected Contribution*: High ($+0.05$ Pearson).
   - *Rationale*: Inter-molecular hydrogen bonds are key physical drivers of binding affinity.
2. **Atom Hybridization & Valence States**:
   - *Expected Contribution*: Moderate ($+0.02$ Pearson).
   - *Rationale*: Captures local ligand molecular mechanics.
3. **Solvent Accessibility Surface Area (SASA)**:
   - *Expected Contribution*: High ($+0.04$ Pearson).
   - *Rationale*: Models hydrophobic effect (desolvation entropy).
